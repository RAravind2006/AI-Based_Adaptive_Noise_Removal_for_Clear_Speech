Architecture

How It Works

Incoming WAV or MP3 recordings are decoded, converted to mono at the model's target sample rate, and analyzed in both time and frequency domains. The deep learning speech enhancement pipeline suppresses stationary and non-stationary background noise while preserving vocal harmonics and natural speech intelligibility.

Deep Learning & Signal Pipeline

  • Pre-processing: Resampling, peak normalization, and STFT framing via Librosa & PyTorch.
  • AI Noise Suppression: Pretrained neural denoiser / PyTorch adaptive spectral masking, with Librosa + Spectral Gating fallback.
  • Evaluation & Visuals: Honest duration, processing latency, noise-floor reduction in dB, optional reference SNR, and Matplotlib plots.
